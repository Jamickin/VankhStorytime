# Chapter 4.1.2: The Transmission

"Every cause has its effect; every effect has its cause; everything happens according to law; chance is but a name for law not recognized."

---

[[Pro]] had two pulses.

He spread his non-standard diagnostic tools across the technical observer console and connected them to every output channel he could reach from this seat: the vessel's general communication array, his maintenance network upload credentials, the independent contractor relay he had used to route his mother's medicine shipments, the emergency broadcast register that any vessel operating in settled space was required to maintain even if it was never expected to use it. The channels opened one by one, each one logged by the system in the manifest record that [[Lin]]'s office would eventually pull.

He didn't care about that. She had already made her calculation. The manifest record was no longer the threat.

He opened the [[data crystal]] display and compressed the full archive — all four hundred and twelve data structures — into the diagnostic system's outbound buffer. Then he opened a recording channel and dictated into it, addressing no one in particular and everyone he knew.

"This is Technician P-R-O-4-7-9, Drive Coupling Specialist, [[Cicillian Industries]] independent contractor, transmitting from CCLLIAN-ROUTE-9-ALPHA in transit to Sector 7. The following is a record of what I have found in the last three Parts, and what I believe is about to happen."

He described the [[data crystal]] — his mother's archive, the demographic records, the impossibility of population figures that never changed across wars and plagues. He described the mathematical precision in drive readings that no natural system could hold. He described the secondary harmonic, the scanner work in his quarters, the figure at [[Relay Station One]] and what it transmitted. He described the trajectory of this vessel, which was not a docking approach but a direct heading at the third [[Vankh spheres|Vankh sphere]] at impact velocity.

He compressed his resonance data into the buffer and attached it — the fragmentary readings from the Sector 7 coupling bay, the forty-seven seconds of measurement through the wrong protocol that had still yielded enough to demonstrate that the frequencies at the sphere's surface did not exist in any catalogue. He attached his biometric logs from the deployment, which showed the breathing deviation and the neural pattern the system had classified as "unclassified resonance event" and which was, he could now describe precisely, the moment of direct transmission from a [[Vankh'Ari]] at seventeen kilometers' distance.

He addressed the transmission to his mother's medical archive tag — C-A-R-4-5-8, [[Helix Habitat]] patient registry. If the temporal stabilizers were working, she would have a clear interval. She would understand what the demographic records meant before he could explain it, because she had already understood it and spent her career proving it and pressed the crystal into his hands to make sure the understanding survived her.

He addressed a copy to the [[Mechanic]]'s registration code at [[Nexus Station]], because she was the person in his life most likely to know what to do with information that no institutional channel would accept.

He addressed a copy to the captain of the independent vessel — the one who had said "we remember the ones who help" — because the independent network was the only communications infrastructure that had not been routed through Cicillian oversight since the new contractor monitoring policy, and because what was in this transmission needed a path that [[Lin]]'s review queue couldn't intercept before it propagated.

The buffer was full. The output channels were open.

He ran the transmission. He watched the confirmation logs as they returned: the Helix medical archive, received; the Nexus Station independent registry, acknowledged; the emergency broadcast channel, active.

The independent relay came back last with a simple handshake code: *received, routing, no response required.* The kind of confirmation that meant someone had built the relay specifically to handle transmissions that weren't supposed to exist, and had done it well enough that the confirmation itself was untraceable.

One pulse.

VVVVVANKH.

[[Pro]] closed the output channels and set his tools aside and sat with his hands folded on the console in front of him. He thought about his mother receiving the archive and understanding that someone had finally read it the way it was meant to be read. He thought about the [[Mechanic]] opening the resonance data and finding the frequencies that didn't belong in any catalogue, which was her kind of problem to be given. He thought about the captain, in whatever sector his vessel was crossing, receiving a data packet that would route to the people and places that needed it — the way good tools find their way to good hands if you put them in the independent network and let them travel.

He thought about [[Lin]], whose review queue would eventually receive the manifest record for this transmission and understand that it had already left the vessel, that the elimination she had designed so precisely had produced documentation rather than silence. He did not think about this with anger. He understood it the way he understood a drive system: the components had performed exactly as designed. She had been designed by the same system that had designed everything else.

The third [[Vankh spheres|Vankh sphere]] was visible now in the forward viewport — perfect, absolute, occupying the center of his field of vision with the completeness of something that had always been there and was now finished pretending to be backdrop.

The secondary harmonic was fully present.

[[Pro]] adjusted his breathing into the third pattern without deciding to. Even. Both sides. In phase with the Pulse rather than synchronized to it or counter-phased against it. His mother's rhythm, which she had never put in words, which he had not understood when she showed it to him and which he understood now completely: not a technique. A relationship. Something you stopped doing to the frequency and started allowing.

He had transmitted everything he knew. It was outside this vessel now, traveling at the speed of the independent network, which was slower than FTL and faster than the death of information. Whatever happened in the next few seconds, the record was no longer contained in any single location.

He turned his full attention to the sphere.
