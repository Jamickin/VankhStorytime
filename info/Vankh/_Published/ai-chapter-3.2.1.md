# Chapter 3.2.1: The Archive

"Everything is dual; everything has poles; everything has its pair of opposites; like and unlike are the same."

---

He had already seen the population figures. He did not need to see them again.

[[Pro]] set the display to the lower archive layers — his mother's [[data crystal]] indexed by depth as well as category, its oldest records at the base of the structure, laid down first and buried under everything she had built on top of them. The deeper he went, the older the material, and the older the material, the less it resembled the factional histories he had grown up reading. Pre-Consolidation notation. Pre-standardization formats. The data structures his mother had learned to parse over two decades of archival work, reading the grain of the original filing beneath the corporate reformatting that had come later.

He went down until the [[Cicillian Industries]] certification marks stopped appearing on the documents. Then further, until the [[Church of Purity]] did not exist as a reference category because it had not yet been named. Then further still, into the deep historical substrate where the records predated the [[Cicillian]]-[[Purist]] divide by hundreds of [[Vankh-Sett]]s and the language of the documents had not yet been shaped by either side's requirements.

The [[Vankh spheres]] appeared in the third document he opened.

Not as infrastructure. Not as divine instruments. The document was a technical survey, carefully enumerated, produced by an institution that no longer existed and had never been affiliated with either faction. And in it the spheres were called something he had no prior reference for: _karreth_. A word from no language he recognized, filed in a footnote as a transliteration of the original term. The survey described their placement as deliberate. Described their function as: "the walls of what is permitted."

He read it again.

Not the walls of settled space — not a description of the transit network, not a technical assessment of the pulse-generation system that made [[FTL]] travel possible. The walls of what is permitted. A category distinct from what is possible or what is known, carrying within it the implication that something beyond the walls was not absent but excluded. That someone had decided, at some point, on the boundary.

He opened three more documents from the same period. They confirmed it from different angles: a philosophical record, a population management ledger, an administrative correspondence that mentioned the spheres in passing as contextual detail so settled as to require no explanation. One correspondent's phrase, repeated twice: "the seam between what has been built and what lies beyond the building." The same structure. Inside and outside. A construction that had been given walls.

The last document from that layer — the furthest he could reach with the archival indices his mother had built — was fragmented, its data structure damaged in a way that the recovery algorithms could only partially address. The readable sections gave him no attribution. No faction marking. No institutional header. Just the record itself, and the record documented the routine placement of people into the population as administrative maintenance. The same intake cycle designation repeated across multiple entries, spanning decades. The same notation. The same structure.

_The counting of the ones-who-were-placed continues as it has always continued. The intake cycles proceed. We remain._

He sat for a long time without moving the display.

Then he ran his own designation in the search index.

P-R-O-4-7-9. It returned immediately — not from the old records, from the standard corporate layer, the certification pathway his mother had accumulated alongside everything else. Intake date. Original placement location. Certification pathway. Enhancement record. Work history. The file was complete and consistent and recorded everything about his professional existence.

No birth. No parents. An administrative entry.

He stared at the intake date. He had always known when he had entered the certification system — the Academy had his records from that point forward. He had never thought to ask what had preceded the intake entry, because intake entries were the beginning. They were where you started in the system. There was nothing before them by definition.

There was nothing before this one either.

He ran his mother's designation.

C-A-R-4-5-8. Intake date, forty-one Vankh-Setts before his own. Placement location: [[Helix Habitat]], residential sector four. Enhancement record. Certification pathway. Work history. A full professional file, beginning with the same structure his began with, the same absence of anything before the entry's first line.

No birth record. No medical record from before the intake date. No family documentation predating the entry. Nothing that would exist for a person who had been born into the world — the endless peripheral documentation of an actual arrival, the small administrative traces that accumulate around a life before it is old enough to generate professional records of its own.

Just: she was in it, on this date, at this location.

He closed his mother's file.

He did not open his father's.

He knew what it would say. He knew the intake date would be there, and the placement location, and the certification pathway, and nothing before the first line. He knew because the system was consistent — he had been reading consistent systems for eleven years, and a consistent system produces the same result every time you check it. He did not need to check it. He had enough.

He powered down the crystal display and sat in his quarters in the dark. The [[Vankh pulse]] moved through the station structure around him at its usual interval, patient and enormous, the rhythm of the walls of what was permitted. The secondary harmonic ran beneath it, present as it always was, familiar now in the way things become familiar when they have been with you long enough that you no longer notice the moment you stopped being startled by them.

He was a man who had been placed. His mother was a woman who had been placed. Placed into the population the way a component is placed into a drive assembly — with a function, a designated location, a record of installation that began at the moment of installation and not before.

The question he could not answer yet was the question of who had done the placing, and what for, and whether the intake cycles — which the fragment said were continuing, which the fragment said had always been continuing — were still running, and what they produced at the end, and whether the walls of what was permitted existed to keep something in or to keep something out or both simultaneously, as a wall generally did.

He sat in the dark for the rest of that cycle and did not reach for any instrument, and the harmonic was there beneath everything, patient and specific, asking nothing, waiting for the moment when he would be ready to let it answer.
